Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

What we collect

Club admins: your email address, club name, and the branding you configure. Members: the entry name and member name you type when submitting picks — members never create accounts or provide email addresses. Payments: handled entirely by Stripe; ClubMajors never sees or stores card numbers. We also keep standard technical logs needed to run the service.

How we use it

To run your club's pools: showing standings to your club, emailing admins sign-in links and service notices, and processing the club's software fee. Member names are visible to the club's members and golf shop on the leaderboard — that's the point of a pool. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Who processes it

We use Supabase (database and authentication), Netlify (hosting), Stripe (payments), and Resend (email delivery). Score data comes from sports-data providers and involves no personal information.

Retention and deletion

Pool data is kept so clubs can view past results. Want your name removed from an entry, or a club account deleted? Email support@clubmajorsgolf.com and we'll take care of it promptly.

Marketing email

If we ever email you commercially, it will include a working unsubscribe option, and opting out won't affect your service.

Changes

Updates will be posted here with a new date.
